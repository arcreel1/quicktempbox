Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 19, 2026

Overview

QuickTempBox provides temporary, disposable email addresses for short-term use. This policy explains what data is handled and how long it is retained.

Data We Process

When you generate a temporary email address, inbox messages are retrieved from a third-party email provider API to display them in your session.

  • Temporary email address generated for your session
  • Incoming message metadata and content for viewing
  • Basic browser storage items needed for active session functionality

Data Retention

QuickTempBox is built for temporary use. Email inboxes are short-lived and are not intended for long-term storage.

  • Temporary inbox data expires automatically by provider policy
  • Local session values can be removed by deleting the generated inbox or clearing browser data
  • No account registration is required for core usage

No Permanent Mail Logs

QuickTempBox does not maintain a permanent, user-identity based mail archive for this disposable service. Do not use temporary inboxes for sensitive or long-term communications.

Third-Party Services

This project uses external services such as analytics providers and disposable email APIs. Their own privacy policies and retention practices may apply.

Security Notice

Use temporary email only for low-risk tasks such as signups or trial verifications. Never use disposable inboxes for banking, legal, healthcare, or critical account recovery workflows.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the project maintainer using the contact links available on the homepage.
